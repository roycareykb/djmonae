01 — Origin

Musical
Inheritance

Music isn't just what DJ Monae does — it's her inheritance. Born into a family of musical excellence, she carries the DNA of her father, a touring musician who opened for legends like Midnight Star, who introduced her to the stage before she could even walk. From sitting on his lap at the piano to humming melodies down the hallway, music was never just background noise — it was home.

Her mother, the spiritual anchor of the family, rooted DJ Monae in the church, where she first discovered the power of her voice. There, she learned that music was more than performance — it was expression, healing, connection. From gospel harmonies to soul-stirring sets, DJ Monae doesn't just play music — she lives it.

Mission

"To create transformative musical experiences that connect souls, move bodies, and unify communities through the healing power of sound and authentic human connection."
